FAQ

Good to know

What does “Available now” mean?

The item has already landed and is in stock in our (South Africa) or (Zimbabwe) hub. It's delivered within 1–4 working days, or you can collect.

What does “Ships in ~2 weeks” mean?

We buy the item in the USA after you order and ship it to you. Most orders arrive in about 2 weeks. We ask for a deposit up front and the balance on arrival.

Why is the USA price lower?

Ordering from the USA lets us ship your item with our regular consolidated shipment, so we pass on a saving compared with landed stock.

Which currency do I pay in?

Switch between USD and ZAR at the top of the page. Customers in South Africa usually pay in ZAR and customers in Zimbabwe in USD. ZAR prices are based on a rate of and may change with the exchange rate.

What's the difference between Grade A and Grade B refurbished?

Grade A looks close to new with little or no visible wear. Grade B may have light cosmetic marks. Both are fully tested, work perfectly and come with a 6-month Crate warranty.

Will US electronics work here?

Yes. We supply the right plug adapter or converter with every item that needs one, and unlocked phones work on South African and Zimbabwean networks.

Can I return an item?

Unopened beauty products and electronics can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Faulty items are repaired or replaced under warranty.
