FAQ

What does “Ready now” mean?

The item is already in stock at our (South Africa) or (Zimbabwe) hub. We deliver within 1–4 working days, or you can collect it.

What does “~2 weeks” mean?

We buy the item in the USA after you order and ship it to you. Most orders arrive in about 2 weeks. You pay a deposit up front and the balance on arrival.

Why is the USA order price lower?

Your item travels with our regular shipment, so you save compared with landed stock.

Can I pay in rand or dollars?

Yes. Switch currency at the top of the page. Rand prices use a rate of and may change with the exchange rate.

What do refurbished grades mean?

Grade A looks close to new. Grade B may have light marks. Both are fully tested and come with a 6-month warranty.

Will US electronics work here?

Yes. We include the right plug adapter or converter, and unlocked phones work on South African and Zimbabwean networks.

Can I return something?

Unopened items can be returned within 7 days of delivery. Faulty items are repaired or replaced under warranty.
